A free reader app for Archive of Our Own (AO3).

Built because other AO3 apps charge money for features that should be free.

<b>Features:</b>
<ul>
<li>Custom library with favorites</li>
<li>Category management and organization</li>
<li>Reading history with filters</li>
<li>Native AMOLED support</li>
<li>Offline reading</li>
</ul>

<b>No subscriptions, no ads, ever.</b>
